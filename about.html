About Wheels Nepal Enduro

Events by Wheels Adventure Nepal, based in Pokhara, Nepal.

Who we are

Motorsport, camping and culture, together

We organise events that combine national-level motorsport with camping, live music, local food and outdoor experiences, and we are building out bike rides and camping from Pokhara.

To establish Nepal's premier annual enduro and adventure festival while promoting tourism, motorsports and local communities.

Our objective

What we are working towards

To create a world-class enduro and adventure festival that promotes tourism, motorsports and community growth, while delivering an unforgettable experience for riders, visitors and local businesses.

  • Promote adventure tourism.
  • Develop enduro motorsports.
  • Support local businesses.
  • Create an international-standard festival.
  • Inspire youth participation.
