About the event

Mud, mountains
& music.

Wheels Nepal Enduro is envisioned as Nepal's biggest bike and music festival — a national enduro race, three days of camping, live music, bonfire, adventure tourism, local food, exhibitions and outdoor experiences.

Our Vision

To establish Nepal's premier annual enduro and adventure festival while promoting tourism, motorsports and local communities.

Our objective

A world-class enduro and adventure festival that promotes tourism, motorsports and community growth — delivering an unforgettable experience for riders, visitors and local businesses.

  • Promote adventure tourism
  • Develop enduro motorsports
  • Support local businesses
  • Create an international-standard festival
  • Inspire youth participation
Tentative schedule

Three days,
one basecamp.

Registration through camping on day one, qualifying and adventure activities on day two, and the enduro finals on day three.

Day 01

Arrival

Registration, camping check-in, expo village and live music opens the festival.

Day 02

Qualifying

Qualifying heats, adventure activities, evening concert and bonfire under the stars.

Day 03

Finals

Enduro finals, prize ceremony and closing — Nepal's champion enduro rider crowned.

The route

Built into
the hillside.

A looping mud circuit carved into the Hemja hillside, with the expo village, pit lane and grandstand banking at its centre — engineered for both racers and spectators.
